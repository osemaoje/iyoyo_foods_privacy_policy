Privacy Policy

How the Iyoyo Foods mobile application handles personal information and payments.

Effective Date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Iyoyo Foods mobile application, package name com.ojestore.payment (“the App”). It explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to users.

Please read this policy carefully. The information and services available through the App may change over time, and this policy may be updated accordingly.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may collect or process the following categories of information:

Account and Contact Information

  • Name or account profile details
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address and other details you provide for an order

Orders and Transactions

  • Items ordered, quantities, order history, and order status
  • Delivery instructions and information needed to fulfil your order
  • Payment status, transaction reference, amount, currency, and related payment confirmation details

Usage and Technical Information

The App or its supporting services may process information such as app interactions, device and app version details, diagnostic information, crash reports, and basic technical logs where these features are enabled.

2. Payments Through Flutterwave

The App integrates with Flutterwave to facilitate payments. When you make a payment, Flutterwave and relevant payment partners may process information needed to initiate, authenticate, complete, verify, and support the transaction. This may include your name, contact details, transaction amount, payment method details, and transaction identifiers, depending on the payment method you select.

Payment-card information: Payment details may be collected directly by Flutterwave or its payment partners through their payment interface. We do not intend to collect or store full payment-card details in the App's own systems. Please confirm this statement matches your actual implementation before publishing this policy.

Flutterwave processes information under its own privacy terms and applicable requirements. For more information, review Flutterwave's website and the privacy information provided during checkout. We may receive transaction confirmations and references so that we can reconcile payments and fulfil orders.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts, where supported
  • Receive, process, deliver, and provide support for food orders
  • Initiate and confirm payments and manage refunds or transaction disputes
  • Communicate order updates and important service notices
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App
  • Detect suspicious activity, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary to operate the App, including with:

  • Flutterwave and payment partners to process and verify payments
  • Delivery or fulfilment providers, where applicable, to deliver orders
  • App infrastructure and technology providers that host, secure, or support the App, if used
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security

Third-party providers process information under their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, manage orders and transactions, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is held.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where consent from a parent or guardian is legally required, it must be obtained before collecting a child's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the details below so we can review the matter.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may connect to third-party services, including Flutterwave and any hosting, analytics, authentication, or diagnostic services enabled in the App. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the relevant policies when using those services.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Some information may need to be retained to complete transactions or meet legal obligations.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
